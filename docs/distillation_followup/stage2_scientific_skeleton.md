# Stage 2 Scientific Skeleton Freeze

## Status

This is the authoritative prose statement of the Stage 2 scientific-skeleton freeze.

This is a **partial scientific-skeleton freeze**. It does not declare the full experimental protocol numerically final, production-ready, or ready for endpoint-producing scientific execution.

Stage 3 has not begun.

## Frozen scientific skeleton

### FS-001 — Exact research question

> Under a fixed component basis, intervention and evaluation protocol, does the grokking-associated change in sparse-circuit recoverability follow the teacher function, vary across distilled realizations of that function, or arise from the distillation and discovery procedures themselves?

**Claim limit:** Freezes the scientific question only; it does not freeze later numeric, roster, or production choices.

### FS-002 — Experimental hierarchy

The hierarchy is teacher seed -> phase within teacher -> distillation condition -> student initialization -> discovery method -> thresholds/circuits, with phase repeated within teacher.

**Claim limit:** No lower level may be treated as the population-level unit.

### FS-003 — Population-level unit

Teacher seed is the population-level unit.

**Claim limit:** Student initializations are not independent population replicates.

### FS-004 — Student-initialization interpretation

Student initialization is realization variation conditional on a teacher.

**Claim limit:** It measures conditional realization variation rather than population-level replication.

### FS-005 — Repeated-measurement layers

Methods, thresholds, and circuits are repeated measurements within the higher-level hierarchy.

**Claim limit:** They are not independent population units.

### FS-006 — Hard and soft estimands

Hard-target and soft-target students are separate estimands and are never pooled.

**Claim limit:** Pooling hard and soft students into one estimand is prohibited.

### FS-007 — Direct teacher evaluation

Every selected teacher is evaluated directly under the identical circuit protocol.

**Claim limit:** Teacher circuit behavior may not be inferred only through distilled students.

### FS-008 — Hard-target eligibility

Hard-target eligibility requires 100% reproduction of teacher argmax decisions over all 12,769 inputs.

**Claim limit:** Any hard-target student failing even one teacher argmax decision is ineligible.

### FS-009 — Student circuit-fidelity reference

Student circuit fidelity is relative to the student's own dense outputs.

**Claim limit:** It is not defined relative to the teacher's dense outputs.

### FS-010 — Proposed primary fidelity family

Centred-logit predictive fidelity is the proposed primary fidelity family, including per-input additive-logit gauge removal.

**Claim limit:** Implementation details, threshold, and precision remain unresolved until Stage 12.

### FS-011 — Endpoint 1 definition

Endpoint 1 is the smallest recovered qualifying component proportion under a specified procedure, with the intact mask at proportion 1.0 as fallback.

**Claim limit:** The fallback keeps Endpoint 1 defined when sparse recovery fails.

### FS-012 — Endpoint 1 interpretation limit

Endpoint 1 is procedure-relative and only an upper bound on the unknown globally minimal sufficient proportion.

**Claim limit:** It must not be described as the true or globally minimal sufficient circuit size.

### FS-013 — Endpoint 2 definition and interpretation

Endpoint 2 is a procedure-relative packing lower bound under a specified procedure, including zero when no qualifying circuit is recovered.

**Claim limit:** It must not be described as the true number of distinct circuits or an exhaustive circuit count.

### FS-014 — Method-budget interpretation

Discovery methods receive method-specific native budgets plus a common final exact-mask-evaluation allowance, and primary phase/student comparisons are interpreted within method.

**Claim limit:** Raw packing counts across methods are not described as perfectly resource-matched.

### FS-015 — Outcome-category status

The six stated outcome categories are interpretations, not predictions.

**Claim limit:** The protocol does not prospectively predict which category will occur.

### FS-016 — Fourier interchange scope

Fourier interchange is a gated secondary experiment with all five required controls.

**Claim limit:** It is not a primary endpoint and does not establish uniqueness.

### FS-017 — Gated extensions

Entropy estimation, cross-task breadth, general theory, and conditional atlases are gated extensions outside the core critical path.

**Claim limit:** They cannot silently become required primary analyses.

### FS-018 — Method-development firewall

Pre-freeze endpoint-producing method-development output must be excluded, registered, and regenerated after freeze before definitive use.

**Claim limit:** Such output may not select phase checkpoints, thresholds, component caps, overlap cutoffs, student counts, methods, budgets, or other scientific protocol values.
