# Stage 11 post-red-team design resolution

**Status:** candidate scientific interfaces at Barrier 1
**Scientific execution:** no
**Production values frozen:** no
**Historical Stage 2 register mutated:** no

## Purpose

Stage 11 converts the completed scientific red-team into prospective interfaces
for Stages 12--14. It does not run teacher training, student training, circuit
discovery, Fourier scientific execution, or definitive endpoint analysis.

The machine-readable authorities are:

- `followup/manifests/stage11_red_team_resolution_v1.json`
- `followup/configs/stage11_post_red_team_design_candidates_v1.json`

## Population and phase hierarchy

Teacher seed remains the population-level unit. The protected primary study is
modular addition modulo 113. The candidate direction is to expand beyond the
five predecessor teachers toward approximately fifteen teachers if they can be
constructed prospectively, but the exact roster and exact count remain open.

Phase selection must use training metrics only. Matched availability is
required, and unavailable teacher-phase cells remain explicit reportable
states rather than being fabricated, replaced silently, or imputed.

## Task scope

- **Task 1:** protected full-domain modular addition modulo 113.
- **Task 2:** reduced modular multiplication replication.
- **Task 3:** capacity-contingent reduced modular-polynomial replication.

Task 2 production modulus remains open. Task 3 formula, coefficients, exponents,
modulus, and exact scope remain open until prospective freeze. Task 3 may not be
chosen using candidate scientific outcomes. Modular subtraction does not count
as the intended task-breadth repair.

## Architecture panel

The predecessor-matched architecture remains canonical. The candidate design
allows a systematic panel of at most five student architecture families with a
balanced sparse assignment, not a literal full factorial.

Every architecture must expose parameter count, searchable-component count, and
component-type counts. Architecture results are external-validity evidence and
conditional repeated factors. A heterogeneous panel does not identify an
isolated causal effect of depth, width, or another architectural property.

The exact architecture roster and assignment remain open.

## Component bases

Required candidate basis families are:

1. canonical attention heads plus individual MLP neurons;
2. pre-output-projection attention coordinates;
3. prospectively seeded balanced neuron blocks;
4. limited fixed orthogonal rotations in eligible activation subspaces;
5. parameter-weighted and component-type-stratified accounting.

Exact coordinate definitions, block counts, block policies, partition seeds,
rotation counts, and rotation seeds remain open. Claims are limited to tested
bases and partitions.

## Endpoint hierarchy

Endpoint 1 remains primary: the smallest exactly evaluated component proportion
satisfying the frozen centred-logit fidelity rule. The intact mask is always
included. It is a procedure-relative recovered upper bound, not a claim of the
global minimum sufficient circuit.

Endpoint 2 remains a key secondary procedure-relative packing lower bound unless
preproduction validation before Stage 13 prospectively justifies co-primary
status. It is not a mechanism count or true packing number.

The primary fidelity threshold, fidelity frontier, component cap, overlap rule,
cutoff, and packing solver remain open. Existing exact ledgers must be reused for
reducer-only sensitivities where valid.

## Discovery and packing calibration

Protected production requires two algorithmically distinct discovery families:

- inherited discrete greedy/diversity machinery;
- an independent continuous or stochastic sparse-mask method.

A restart wrapper around the inherited method does not satisfy independence.
Method-native budgets remain non-equivalent and open. A common exact-evaluation
allowance is separately accounted and remains open.

Packing requires four distinct calibration layers:

1. size/type-matched combinatorial floor;
2. ordinary-restart baseline;
3. local fidelity-retaining perturbation;
4. exact or certified near-exact tractable feasible-region calibration.

The tractable calibration problem is technical calibration, not an additional
population replicate. Exact model/task choice, mask universe, stopping
certificate, draw counts, and reportable minimum remain open.

## Fourier causal interchange

Fourier interchange is a registered key secondary analysis and is executed
regardless of primary result direction. Pair selection may not use candidate
scientific outcomes.

Required controls are wrong Fourier mode, shuffled coefficients, mismatched
input, equal-norm random state, and unaligned ordinary activation patching.
Evidence for a shared causal abstraction requires outperforming every
capacity-matched registered control. It does not uniquely identify an algorithm.

Exact pair roster, intervention location, alignment rule, capacity match, and
outcome remain open until freeze.

## Tiering and resource contingencies

A literal full factorial is rejected.

- **Tier 1:** protected primary matrix.
- **Tier 2:** protected external-validity and interpretation minimum.
- **Tier 3:** optional breadth only after Tier 1 completeness and the protected
  Tier 2 minimum are secure.

Work is shed in reverse priority: Tier 3 first, then unprotected Tier 2 work.
Protected work cannot be displaced by optional breadth.

The resource plan must contain qualified-Symbolica, Symbolica-unavailable,
school-Mac, and incomplete-campaign recovery branches. School-Mac priority is
primary recovery, reproduction/integrity, Task 2 after protected requirements,
and Task 3 only after the completeness gate.

Concurrency, retry caps, storage quotas, exact audit/export reserve, and
post-submission rebuttal-reserve amount remain open for Stages 13--14.

## Analysis and claims boundary

Hard and soft students remain separate estimands. Failed attempts,
ineligibility, unavailable cells, search failure, and budget censoring remain
visible. Teacher seed is the effective population unit for phase/function
contrasts.

Permitted claims are procedure-relative and limited to tested architectures,
bases, methods, tasks, and registered causal abstractions. Prohibited claims
include global circuit minimality, enumeration of mechanisms, true packing
number, pseudo-replication from lower-level jobs, isolated architecture
causality from a heterogeneous panel, universal basis invariance, equal compute
across unlike discovery methods, and uniquely identified algorithms from
Fourier interchange.

## Barrier 1 handoff

Stage 12 receives interfaces, not production settings. Stage 13 must freeze all
production-blocking values before Stage 15. Stage 14 must qualify the actual
backend and operational campaign. Stage 15 remains unstarted.
